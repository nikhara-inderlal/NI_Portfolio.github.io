What I do

How a business question becomes production reporting

Every case study in this portfolio follows the same arc. This is the part that matters more than which tool was used.

01

Discover and clarify

I run the requirements sessions with subject-matter experts myself: what decision is this for, who reads it, and what would a useful answer actually look like. Then I investigate the sources, grain, quality and existing logic to find out what the data can honestly support.

02

Design the analysis

Defining the metric logic, the comparison that is fair, and the modelling structure underneath it. This is where a maths background earns its place, because most reporting disputes turn out to be definitional rather than technical.

03

Build and validate

Building the data, semantic, reporting or workflow layer, then reconciling results across source, curated and report layers, testing the logic and chasing anomalies to a cause before anyone else sees the number.

04

Deliver into production

Leading the stakeholder walkthrough, incorporating feedback, documenting the logic, securing sign-off and deploying validated work through TEST to PROD, so it becomes something the business uses rather than something that was demonstrated once.
