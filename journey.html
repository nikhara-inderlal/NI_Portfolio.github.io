Journey

How I got here.

Operational data analysis first, then the mathematics, then two years teaching statistics, then consulting. Each step is why I work the way I do now.

2016 to 2021 · Optimized Routing Solutions

Telecommunications Network Analyst, part-time. Monitored international switching infrastructure, analysed traffic, capacity and performance trends, and investigated routing and traffic anomalies. My relationship with operational data started here, several years before consulting: recurring quantitative reporting with validation checks, used for network monitoring and commercial follow-up.

2021 to 2023 · Mathematics and Statistical Modelling

BSc at Macquarie University, Mathematics major and Statistical Modelling minor. WAM 83.6, and a Certificate of Highest Achievement for MATH3599 PACE. Applied statistics, probability and linear models are the foundation I still reason from when defining a metric or deciding whether a comparison is fair.

2023 to 2025 · Macquarie University

Lead Sessional and Teaching Associate in Business Statistics and Insights, and Calculus and Linear Algebra, for classes of 30+ students, alongside a Research Assistant role in Statistics. Two years explaining hypothesis testing, confidence intervals and regression to people who did not want a statistics lecture. That is the same skill a stakeholder walkthrough needs, and it is where I learned it.

Mar 2025 · MIP (Aust) Pty Ltd

Joined as Associate Consultant and Analytics Engineer through The Data School, moving quickly from training into client delivery across Power BI, Tableau, SQL, Alteryx and Tableau Prep.

2025 · Client delivery

Consecutive engagements across telematics, food manufacturing, engineering services, QSR and franchise, education and the not-for-profit sector. Roles ranged from data engineer to project lead of a six-person analytics team, and included proposing and building a fuel-efficiency solution outside my assigned scope that was selected as joint winner of an internal dashboard challenge.

Sep 2025 to present · Large Australasian Healthcare Organisation

Enterprise analytics across Finance, Marketing, Clinical Assurance and People & Culture for a large Australasian healthcare organisation. Working inside a governed 70-table Gold-layer Direct Lake model, owning delivery from SME requirements through modelling, validation, documentation, sign-off and production release, and extending into Power Platform workflow automation and enterprise data governance.
