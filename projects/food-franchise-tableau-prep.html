Franchise Sales & Basket Analytics at Oakberry

Making monthly franchise reporting scalable across 40+ locations, then using the clean data to give the client its first view of what customers actually buy together.

SectorOakberry, a global QSR brand with 600+ stores across 35+ countries
RoleAnalytics Consultant / Tableau Developer
EngagementMexican operation, 40+ franchise locations
ToolsTableau Prep, Tableau, Excel VBA, Hyper, LOD expressions, Parameters

Context

Oakberry is a global brand with more than 600 stores across 35+ countries. This engagement supported the Mexican operation across more than 40 franchise locations, working with Spanish-language source data. Monthly reporting was assembled from 38 separate Excel source files.

Business problem

Two problems sat on top of each other. The obvious one was that rebuilding reporting from 38 differently-shaped Excel files every month did not scale and could not support consistent comparison between locations.

The less obvious one was what that manual process was preventing. Because every month was consumed by assembling the data, nobody was analysing what it contained. The franchise had no visibility into basket behaviour: which toppings went with which products, how often, and what that was worth.

Approach

I led the data preparation in Tableau Prep, cleaning, pivoting and aggregating sales data from the 38 Excel files. The structural blocker was that products and toppings were stacked across rows in a single field, which makes reliable product-to-topping mapping impossible. I developed an Excel VBA row-ID solution to correctly map products to toppings, which is what unlocked downstream basket and combination analysis.

I then implemented wildcard unioning in Tableau Prep so future store files are integrated automatically, and output clean Hyper extracts for recurring monthly reporting. That turned a monthly rebuild into a repeatable ingestion process.

Modelling & preparation

One long-format fact structure covering store, product, topping, date and amount, with parameters for store and reporting period and LOD expressions so franchise-wide benchmarks stay correct when the view is filtered to a single location. Hyper extracts kept month-end reload fast.

Validation

Checked prepared store totals back against their own raw Excel source figures and confirmed year-on-year calculations against the prior-year data before the dashboards were built on top, since a mapping error in the row-ID logic would have propagated silently into every basket figure.

Stakeholder delivery & handover

Built five dynamic Tableau dashboards optimised for PDF distribution and delivered the reporting experience in Spanish for the Mexican business, working with Spanish-language source data throughout, and secured stakeholder sign-off.

Outcomes

Monthly franchise reporting became repeatable across 40+ locations, with new store files integrating automatically rather than requiring rework. The dashboards covered month-on-month and year-on-year KPIs, product and category performance, delivery versus in-store behaviour, peak days and times and revenue trends.

The more valuable outcome was analytical rather than operational. The work introduced the client's first basket-analysis capability, surfacing previously unavailable insight into topping combinations, topping penetration, average toppings by product type and customer product affinity. Automation made the reporting sustainable; the row-ID mapping made a new class of question answerable at all.

Reflection

The prep flow was the real deliverable, but not only because it saved effort. Solving the stacked product and topping structure was what made basket analysis possible, so a data-preparation decision turned into the client gaining a capability they did not previously have. That is usually where the value in this kind of work sits.
