Problem
Store-level reporting was split across many Excel files, making consistent monthly review slow and fragile.

Business problem

Monthly store reporting was distributed across many spreadsheets and needed a consistent reporting pack.

My role and ownership

Prepared and standardised the source files, shaped the data and built Tableau reporting outputs for store review.

What I built

Used Tableau Prep, Excel VBA and Tableau to create printable dashboards covering KPIs, product analysis, topping analysis and year-on-year trends.

Validation and reliability

Focused on consistency across the file set so stores could be compared using the same definitions.

Outcome

The result was a repeatable reporting process from messy Excel inputs to usable monthly store packs.
