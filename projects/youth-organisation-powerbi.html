National Youth Organisation Reporting Suite

Team-led Power BI reporting project across multiple managerial levels for a national youth organisation.

SectorNot-for-profit / Youth Organisation
RoleProject Lead / Power BI Developer
EngagementTeam-based project engagement
ToolsPower BI, Power Query, Amazon Redshift, Excel documentation

Context

A national youth organisation needed a refreshed reporting suite to support both head-office decision making and the regional managers running local programmes. Existing reporting was inconsistent across regions and depended on heavy manual effort.

Business problem

Head office and regional managers were reporting different totals for the same programmes, and the manual rollup was slow enough that decisions were being made on data that was weeks out of date.

Approach

Coordinated a small project team using Monday.com for transformation tracking, documented requirements per stakeholder group, and ran fortnightly review sessions. Power BI work pulled from Amazon Redshift, with documented Power Query steps and a clear hand-back-to-client process at the end.

Modelling & preparation

One semantic model with consistent programme, region and member dimensions. Page layouts tailored to head office (rollup view) vs regional managers (own-region drill view), but both sharing the same metrics so numbers always reconcile across views.

Validation

Per-region totals reconciled against the regional managers' own manual rollups, walkthroughs with each manager before publishing, and a documented data dictionary handed over so the client could maintain the reports going forward.

Stakeholder delivery & handover

Five-dashboard reporting suite handed over with a complete documentation pack: requirements log, transformation steps, data dictionary, refresh schedule and a written maintenance guide so the client team could carry the work forward.

Outcomes

The organisation now reports on the same numbers from board level down to programme delivery. Manual rework dropped sharply, and the documentation handover meant the client could carry the work forward without depending on the consulting team.

Reflection

Project coordination mattered as much as the BI build. Keeping a single tracker, agreed cadence and named owners stopped the work from sprawling and made the handover clean — the BI work itself was the easier half.
