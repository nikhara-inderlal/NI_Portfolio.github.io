National Youth Organisation Reporting Suite

This is the project I'd point to for leading delivery rather than for dashboard craft. Power BI was the deliverable; running the team, the review loops and the stakeholder conversations was the actual work.

The problem

Managers needed reporting they could use to understand programme activity and outcomes — pulled from multiple operational views in Redshift — with a delivery process that didn't fall apart after the first hand-over.

What I built

Led a team Power BI delivery against Redshift. Power Query for the transformations, structured managerial reporting views in Power BI, and transformation documentation so every step from source to report was traceable. Stakeholder review loops were built into the schedule, not bolted on at the end.

Validation

The validation was as much about people as data — stakeholder reviews at every level, plus transformation tracking so a question like "where does this number come from?" had a clear answer instead of a hunt.

What changed

A reporting suite the managers used, and a delivery process the team could pick up. This was also the case that taught me the most about leading a small group through ambiguity.
