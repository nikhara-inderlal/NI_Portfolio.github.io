Dashboard Week: Rapid Analytics Delivery

Five days. Five fresh data sources. A deadline at the end of each one. Dashboard Week is the Data School exercise that turns out to be as much about judgement as about tools.

The problem

Each day brought a new data source — sometimes API-only, sometimes web-scraped, sometimes a tar of CSVs with no documentation — and a deadline that didn't move. The goal wasn't a perfect dashboard; it was a useful, defensible one by the end of the day.

What I built

Across the week: API access, web scraping, regex cleanup, Tableau Prep, Power BI and Tableau builds. The real skill being practised was deciding what to skip — when to simplify the question, when to switch tools, and when to ship a smaller version of the answer.

Validation

Less a question of reconciling numbers, more a question of being honest about what each dashboard could and couldn't say. Most days the validation step was a sentence in the write-up about what I'd left out and why.

What changed

A small library of rapid prototypes, but the more useful output was the practice itself: getting comfortable making the call on what to ship under a deadline. This is a supporting case — it sits behind the client-style work in the rest of the portfolio.
