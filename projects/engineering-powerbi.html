Tender & Opportunity Analytics at UGL

Helping executives decide which tenders are worth pursuing, using evidence rather than recollection.

SectorUGL, a specialist end-to-end engineering and services provider and market leader in multiple sectors
RoleAnalytics Consultant / Power BI Developer
EngagementExecutive opportunity and tender analytics
ToolsPower BI, Power Query, DAX, Data modelling

Context

UGL is a specialist end-to-end engineering and services provider and a market leader across several sectors. Leadership held detailed opportunity and tender records but had no consolidated view of pipeline health, win-rate performance or how results differed by business unit, region and opportunity size.

Business problem

Pursuit decisions consume real resource. Bidding is expensive, and choosing which opportunities to chase was relying on recollection rather than evidence, because getting the analysis meant commissioning it each time.

The harder analytical problem sat underneath. Historical win rates only help for opportunity types with history. For a new client, sector or contract size, there is no direct precedent, which is exactly where a prioritisation decision is hardest and where a naive model quietly returns nothing useful.

Approach

I built an executive Power BI solution for active opportunity and tender analysis covering expected contract-value exposure, pipeline stages and historical win performance across business units and regions. I developed a tender classification framework and calculated historical win rates across grouped opportunity data, so performance could be read by opportunity type rather than only in aggregate.

To address opportunities without direct historical records, I designed benchmark-based estimation logic using comparable clients, industries and business groupings. That extends win-rate coverage to the opportunities that would otherwise fall outside the analysis, which is the part that makes the tool usable for prioritisation rather than just reporting.

Modelling & preparation

Used Power Query extensively for cleaning, deduplication, grouping, merging and aggregation to create a reliable opportunity dataset from raw records. Built DAX-driven executive KPIs with hierarchical tracking by business unit, sub-unit, region and stage, filtering by source and tender dates, and a dynamic last-refresh indicator so viewers can see data recency.

Validation

Reviewed the win-rate definition and the tender classification framework with stakeholders before the analysis was built on top of them, since a metric like win rate is only as defensible as the agreement on what counts as a win.

Stakeholder delivery & handover

Presented the methodology, assumptions and dashboard to client stakeholders and secured final sign-off. Presenting the assumptions alongside the output mattered here: an estimation model used to prioritise commercial decisions has to be understood by the people acting on it.

Outcomes

Executives can prioritise tender opportunities using evidence-based commercial analysis, with historical win rates, contract-value exposure and benchmark-derived estimates for opportunities without direct history, all readable down through business unit, region and stage.

Reflection

The Power BI development was the smaller half of this project. The useful work was the commercial reasoning: deciding what a comparable client actually is, and how far a benchmark can be stretched before it stops being evidence. Agreeing the win-rate definition up front prevented every future pipeline review from re-litigating the same question.
