Engineering Services Opportunity Tracking

Executives needed to see the shape of the opportunity pipeline without trawling spreadsheets. This was a Power BI build, but the interesting modelling sat in the win-rate and benchmark logic underneath.

The problem

Tender and opportunity information lived in flat operational records. Executives wanted to understand pipeline performance, win-rate patterns and the implied value of work in progress — but the only way to get there was a manual spreadsheet pass every reporting cycle.

What I built

A Power BI model with Power Query transformations on top of the opportunity data, DAX measures for historical win-rate analysis broken out by segment, and benchmark-based estimation logic for opportunity value. The dashboard frames pipeline as a set of patterns to interrogate, rather than a list to read.

Validation

Checked outputs against source categories and against what stakeholders expected to see — so the dashboard reflects the business definition of an opportunity and a win, not just whatever was technically available in the source table.

What changed

The monthly pipeline review now starts from the dashboard. Conversations move faster because everyone is looking at the same view of win-rate and opportunity value.
