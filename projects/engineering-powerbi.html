Business problem

Opportunity and tender information needed to be turned into useful executive reporting rather than a static operational record.

My role and ownership

Prepared the opportunity data, modelled reporting logic and designed dashboard views around pipeline, win-rate and opportunity-value questions.

What I built

Built transformations in Power Query and DAX metrics for historical win-rate patterns, benchmarking and executive summary views.

Validation and reliability

Checked outputs against source categories and stakeholder expectations so the dashboard reflected business definitions rather than only technical fields.

Outcome

The dashboard made commercial opportunity patterns faster to review and easier to discuss with stakeholders.
