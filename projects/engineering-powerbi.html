Engineering Services Opportunity Tracking Dashboard

Executive dashboard for business opportunities, tender performance and historical win-rate analysis.

SectorEngineering Services
RoleData Analyst / Power BI Developer
EngagementProject engagement
ToolsPower BI, Power Query, DAX, Data modelling

Context

Sales leadership had detailed opportunity records in their CRM but no single view of pipeline health, win-rate trend, or how performance differed by sector and opportunity size. Decisions about resource allocation were anecdotal.

Business problem

Leadership couldn't see win-rate trends or sector-level performance without commissioning a custom analysis each time. Pursuit decisions relied on memory and gut rather than evidence.

Approach

Modelled opportunity data into clean fact and dimension tables in Power BI, with date intelligence on opportunity creation and close dates, and built a single executive page plus drilldown pages for sector, stage and value-band analysis.

Modelling & preparation

Star schema with dim_date, dim_sector, dim_stage, fact_opportunity, built from raw opportunity data using Power Query grouping and merging and opportunity classification. DAX measures for win rate (closed-won / closed-total), pipeline value, conversion ratios by stage, and rolling 12-month win-rate trend with sector breakdown. A matrix table presented stage-by-sector conversion, a last-refresh timestamp showed data recency, and benchmarking logic compared current opportunities against historical win-rate patterns to support pursuit prioritisation.

Validation

Reconciled total opportunity counts and values against the CRM's own native reports. Reviewed win-rate definitions with sales leadership to make sure 'closed-lost-no-decision' was being treated consistently across the analysis.

Stakeholder delivery & handover

Walked sales leadership through the executive page and the drilldown structure. Documented the win-rate definition, the data refresh cadence and the CRM-stage mapping so the analysis remained defensible after handover.

Outcomes

Leadership reviews pipeline weekly using one dashboard. Conversations about pursuit decisions now reference the same numbers and the same definitions, and the team can spot win-rate shifts by sector before they show up in revenue.

Reflection

Agreeing the win-rate definition up front saved rework later. It was a small conversation that prevented every future pipeline review from re-litigating the same question.
