Food Manufacturing & Distribution Performance Dashboard

Site-level expense and forecast performance with MTD/YTD metrics, actuals vs forecast and $/tonne analysis.

SectorFood Manufacturing
RoleData Analyst
EngagementProject engagement
ToolsTableau, Alteryx, Calculated fields, KPI design

Context

Operations leaders across multiple manufacturing and distribution sites needed a single weekly view of expense performance against forecast, with the ability to drill into specific cost categories and compare site efficiency on a normalised $/tonne basis.

Business problem

Site managers were each interpreting their own numbers differently and weekly variance reviews were spent reconciling figures rather than discussing causes. Leadership had no consistent benchmark to compare site efficiency.

Approach

Used Alteryx to consolidate forecast, actuals and tonnage extracts from multiple source systems, normalise category labels, and produce a clean reporting-ready dataset. Built the Tableau dashboard around a small set of clear executive KPIs with consistent variance treatment.

Modelling & preparation

Calculated fields for MTD/YTD variance, $/tonne, and forecast-attainment percentage. Reusable parameters for time period and site, with LOD calculations to keep site benchmarks correct under filter changes.

Validation

Variance reconciliation against finance's manual workbook for two reporting periods, edge-case checks on negative actuals and zero-tonne sites, and a walkthrough with the operations lead before publishing.

Stakeholder delivery & handover

Live walkthroughs with site managers and the operations lead, with a short follow-up Q&A session a week later to capture refinements. Dashboard published with a one-page interpretation guide so new viewers could read it without a verbal handover.

Outcomes

Site managers can now review their own performance and benchmark against peer sites in one place. The weekly variance review is shorter and the conversation moves faster from 'what happened' to 'why' and 'what we will do about it'.

Reflection

Restraint on KPI count was the call that mattered most. The initial brief asked for a long list of metrics; agreeing on a smaller, sharper set made the dashboard more useful and the weekly conversation more productive.
