Enterprise Healthcare Reporting Rebuild

I'm the analytics engineer on this rebuild — a Finance & Assurance reporting layer on Microsoft Fabric. Direct Lake semantic model on the inside, Power BI and a bit of Excel on the outside, and a lot of attention to where the numbers come from in between.

The problem

Finance and Assurance reporting was fragmented across earlier systems. KPI logic varied between reports, and the audit path from a source extract to a published figure was hard to follow — so reconciling a number often meant rebuilding it by hand.

What I built

On the curation side: T-SQL and Spark notebooks shaping the reporting tables, plus dimensional extensions to support point-in-time workforce snapshots. On top of that, a shared Direct Lake semantic model with reusable base measures so the same KPI means the same thing in every Power BI and Excel output. Released through a controlled DEV–TEST flow with Azure DevOps and Confluence.

Validation

Reconciliation and tie-outs across source extracts, curated datasets and the final reports. The piece I'm most attached to is the exception reporting for missing reference mappings — payment attributes, staff identifiers, that sort of thing — so a data gap surfaces as a visible exception instead of silently distorting a number further downstream.

What changed

Finance and Assurance teams now read consistent metrics with a clear path from source to figure. The interesting part isn't the dashboards on top — it's the modelling and reconciliation discipline that makes them worth reading.
