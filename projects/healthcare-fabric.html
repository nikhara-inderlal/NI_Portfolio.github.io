Enterprise Healthcare Reporting Rebuild

Supporting a Microsoft Fabric semantic model rebuild for governed enterprise reporting.

SectorHealthcare
RoleAnalytics Engineer
EngagementMulti-month engagement
ToolsMicrosoft Fabric, Power BI, Lakehouse, Direct Lake

Context

The client operated multiple legacy reporting layers with inconsistent metric definitions, no shared dimension model, and a long manual refresh process. Stakeholders across clinical, operational and finance functions could not reconcile the same KPIs across reports.

Approach

The team adopted a Microsoft Fabric Lakehouse + Direct Lake approach. My contribution focused on the semantic layer: preparing and validating fact and dimension tables, supporting reusable DAX measures, and helping define metric ownership so the same number meant the same thing across all reports.

Modelling & preparation

Star-schema model with conformed dimensions for time, site, service line and patient cohort. Fact tables for admissions, episodes, and cost ledger lines. Reusable DAX measures with explicit definitions in the data dictionary. Direct Lake mode chosen for the largest fact tables to keep refresh windows short.

Validation

Numeric reconciliation against the prior reporting layer for a 12-month overlap window, row-count diffs after every transformation step, and stakeholder sign-off per business area before any report was promoted to the published workspace.

Outcomes

Stakeholders across business areas now consume the same conformed metrics. Reduced argument over definitions, faster onboarding of new reports against the shared semantic model, and a clearer audit path from source through to published number.
