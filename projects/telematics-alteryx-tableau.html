Telematics Data Enrichment and Fuel Efficiency Reporting

An Alteryx ETL workflow and Tableau dashboard that enrich raw telematics data with vehicle details, fuel consumption benchmarks and dynamic fuel pricing to surface fuel wastage, inefficient routes and high-usage vehicles.

SectorTransport / Fleet operations
RoleData Analyst / ETL Developer
EngagementProject engagement
ToolsAlteryx, Tableau, Spatial analytics, API integration, Fuel pricing data, Excel

Context

A fleet operation collected raw telematics data across its vehicles, but the records on their own said little about cost or efficiency. Trips, distances and engine events were captured, yet there was no straightforward way to see which vehicles, routes or drivers were burning more fuel than they should, or what that was costing as fuel prices moved.

Business problem

The telematics feed lacked the context needed for decisions: no vehicle reference details, no consumption benchmark to compare against, and no pricing to convert litres into dollars. Without enrichment, the data could not answer practical questions about wastage, inefficient routes, excessive idling or driver-level fuel cost.

Approach

Built an Alteryx ETL workflow to enrich the raw telematics data with vehicle details, fuel consumption benchmarks and dynamic fuel pricing, then shaped a clean reporting-ready dataset for a Tableau dashboard. The enriched model let operations analyse fuel use by vehicle, route and driver rather than reading raw trip logs.

Data enrichment & preparation

The Alteryx workflow joined telematics records to a vehicle reference table for make, model and expected consumption, applied fuel consumption benchmarks so actual use could be compared against expectation, and integrated dynamic fuel pricing so litres translated into cost over time. Spatial analytics supported route-level views, and spreadsheet-based enrichment filled gaps in the reference data before the final dataset was produced.

Fuel pricing & API integration

Dynamic fuel pricing was pulled through an API and aligned to the relevant periods so cost reflected real price movement rather than a flat assumption. Keeping pricing as a managed input meant the same workflow stayed accurate as prices changed, without reworking the model each time.

Reporting

The Tableau dashboard presented fuel efficiency at fleet, route, vehicle and driver level, highlighting fuel wastage, inefficient routes, high-usage vehicles, excessive idling and driver-level fuel cost patterns. Benchmarks made it clear where actual consumption diverged from expectation rather than just showing totals.

Outcomes

Operations could move from raw telematics logs to a clear view of where fuel was being wasted and what it was costing. Inefficient routes, heavy-usage vehicles and excessive idling became visible and comparable, giving a basis for targeted follow-up rather than broad assumptions.

Reflection

The value was in the enrichment, not the raw feed. Joining vehicles, benchmarks and live pricing turned an opaque telematics stream into operational answers, and keeping fuel pricing as a managed input meant the workflow stayed useful as conditions changed.
