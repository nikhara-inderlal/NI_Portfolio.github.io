## Preservation checklist

- Save a PDF copy of every blog page while it is still live.
- Save each blog’s images/screenshots separately, especially dashboard images and tutorial screenshots.
- Copy the original text into a private archive document for your own records.
- Create rewritten versions for your personal website using your current professional tone.
- Add a short note where needed: “Originally written during my Data School training; rewritten and expanded for my portfolio.”
- Use anonymised mock dashboards where the original dashboard or data is not suitable to publish.
- Update your portfolio Writing page to link to both original source pages and your rewritten versions, while originals remain live.
