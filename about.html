How I approach analytics projects

Clarify the decision

Start with the business question, audience, metric definitions and what a useful output needs to change.

Model with intent

Build repeatable structures and reusable measures rather than one-off report logic.

Validate the numbers

Use reconciliation, tie-outs and exception reporting so stakeholders can trust the output.

Design for review

Shape dashboards around how people compare trends, investigate exceptions and explain performance.

Document and hand over

Make definitions, limitations and transformation steps visible so work remains usable.

Communicate clearly

Translate technical choices into plain business implications during walkthroughs and UAT.
