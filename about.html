About

From mathematics and statistical modelling to practical business intelligence.

My background gives me a strong analytical foundation, but my consulting work has taught me that good analytics is also about context, communication, validation and trust.

Foundation

Analytical thinking

I studied mathematics with a statistical modelling focus, which shaped how I approach uncertainty, patterns, assumptions and evidence. That foundation now supports my work in reporting design, metric definition and data validation.

Rather than treating dashboards as visual outputs only, I think carefully about the model and logic behind the report: what is being measured, how it is calculated, and whether stakeholders can trust the result.

Consulting

Business-ready delivery

Through project-based analytics work, I have developed experience across stakeholder requirements, data preparation, semantic modelling, dashboard design, documentation, walkthroughs and handover.

I enjoy work that sits between the technical and business side: translating messy data and broad questions into structured reporting that supports decisions.

Working style

How I approach analytics projects

Understand the question

I begin by clarifying the business purpose, audience, decision context and what a useful output would look like.

Inspect the data

I review source structure, quality issues, grain, relationships and the transformations needed before reporting.

Model with intent

I aim for clear fact/dimension structures, reusable measures and definitions that reduce repeated report-level logic.

Validate the numbers

I reconcile results, test assumptions, document limitations and ensure outputs align with agreed definitions.

Design for users

I build dashboards around how stakeholders actually review performance, compare trends and investigate exceptions.

Document and hand over

I value data dictionaries, transformation notes, metric definitions and walkthroughs so work remains usable after delivery.
