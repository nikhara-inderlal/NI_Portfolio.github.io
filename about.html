About

A quantitative foundation behind practical analytics delivery.

My background in Mathematics and Statistical Modelling shapes how I approach analytics work. It helps me think carefully about metric definitions, assumptions, data quality, modelling structure and whether a number can be trusted before it becomes part of a dashboard or decision process.

Foundation

Mathematics & statistical modelling

My BSc in Mathematics, with a major in Mathematics and a minor in Statistical Modelling, included applied statistics, probability, linear models and applied statistical methods. That foundation supports the way I design metrics, validate results, investigate anomalies and build reporting models that can be explained and defended.

In practice that shows up in metric design, dimensional modelling and validation: I reason about assumptions, uncertainty, distributional behaviour and definitional consistency before treating a KPI as fact, and I’m at home with reconciliation/tie-outs, anomaly investigation and root-cause analysis because those are statistical-thinking problems before they’re engineering ones.

Analytics engineering

Where rigour meets delivery

The quantitative foundation underpins my analytics engineering work: building governed semantic models, curated fact/dimension structures, reusable base measures and audit-logged pipelines in Microsoft Fabric and BigQuery, then translating those into reporting stakeholders can trust and defend.

I enjoy the work that sits between the technical and business side: translating messy data and broad questions into a clean modelling layer, with reconciliation/tie-outs and UAT walkthroughs that make the numbers stand up to scrutiny.

Working style

How I approach analytics projects

Understand the question

I begin by clarifying the business purpose, audience, decision context and what a useful output would look like.

Inspect the data

I review source structure, quality issues, grain, relationships and the transformations needed before reporting.

Model with intent

I aim for clear fact/dimension structures, reusable measures and definitions that reduce repeated report-level logic.

Validate the numbers

I reconcile results, test assumptions, document limitations and ensure outputs align with agreed definitions.

Design for users

I build dashboards around how stakeholders actually review performance, compare trends and investigate exceptions.

Document and hand over

I value data dictionaries, transformation notes, metric definitions and walkthroughs so work remains usable after delivery.
