About

Hi, I'm Nikhara.

I'm an Analytics Engineer and BI Consultant in Sydney. My background is in mathematics and statistical modelling, which is why I tend to care more about how the numbers are built than how the dashboard looks — though I do care about both.

What I do

I work across the full path from raw source to published report: inspecting the data, building the transformations, modelling the semantic layer, designing the dashboard, validating the output, and walking stakeholders through it.

That mix is the part I'm best at — being equally comfortable in SQL and in a UAT meeting.

Where I'm at

Currently an Associate Consultant at MIP / The Data School, on a Microsoft Fabric Finance & Assurance reporting rebuild.

Sydney-based, open to new roles now, full Australian work rights.

How I got here

From maths to consulting analytics.

Maths, statistics and teaching

BSc in Mathematics with a Statistical Modelling minor at Macquarie University, including the MATH3599 PACE Certificate of Highest Achievement. Then a few years teaching Business Statistics, Calculus and Linear Algebra at Macquarie — which is where I learned that being able to explain something is half the job.

The Data School / MIP

Joined The Data School Down Under for the eight-week training programme, then moved into consulting at MIP. Worked across Power BI, Tableau, SQL, Alteryx, Fabric, BigQuery and Tableau Prep — and across healthcare, finance, education, retail, engineering, food manufacturing, not-for-profit and web analytics contexts.

Now

Currently on a Microsoft Fabric Finance & Assurance rebuild as Analytics Engineer / Power BI Developer. Looking for the next role — Analytics Engineer, Data Analytics Engineer, BI Consultant, or anything across the Power BI / Tableau / SQL stack — where the work needs both the modelling underneath and the conversation with stakeholders on top.
