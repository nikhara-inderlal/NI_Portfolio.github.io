Analytics engineering

SQL & database engineering

  • PostgreSQL and DBeaver (online retail SQL audit), SSMS and Power Query (M)
Capability

Data quality & reliability

  • Reconciliation and tie-outs across source, curated and report layers
  • Exception reports for missing reference mappings and integrity gaps
  • Audit logs and rule-based alerts, with false-positive review
  • Data freshness checks and key integrity checks
  • Anomaly investigation and root-cause analysis
  • Validation before stakeholder sign-off, so refresh failures surface rather than fail silently

BI, reporting & decision support

  • Advanced Excel modelling and reporting workbooks
